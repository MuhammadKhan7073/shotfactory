3. PRINT AND PATTERN
- Motif shapes
- Motif size relative to a pillow
- Motif spacing
- Motif density

- Motif alignment and repeat
- Line work: thickness, colour and style

5. FABRIC

No duvet loft. No quilting. No padding. No blanket thickness. No puffy folds.
